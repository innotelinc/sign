DocumentOps · OpenSign, built from source

Every signature stays on your infrastructure.

Sign Platform was the stack's OpenSign-based signing service — client and API built from the bundled source, documents on your own volumes, MongoDB pinned for reliability, identity handled by Cerulean Authentik, and HTTPS terminated at your own edge. It served the public surface until 2026-09-15 and is now retired: the DocumentOps platform is Signara.
